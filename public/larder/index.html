Meet Larder

Every notebook in Series 2 follows one company: Larder, a grocery delivery start-up in Amsterdam that has just promised its investors six cities by the end of 2027. Each book sees the same story through different eyes: the architect's, the product owner's, the project manager's and the account manager's. Read this page first, or come back to it whenever a note mentions someone you don't remember.

1The premise what Larder does

Larder delivers groceries from dark stores: small warehouses closed to the public, where pickers fill each order from the shelves. Couriers on e-bikes take it to the door. Customers order in the app or on the web, with two promises: express (within 30 minutes) and slots (a chosen one-hour window).

The business works. The software is held together by habit. Everything runs in Larder Core, one app written in a hurry by a co-founder who has since left. Now the money has arrived, and with it a deadline.

2How it got here 2024 → 2025

  1. Mar 2024

    Femke and Jasper found Larder in Amsterdam. Jasper, the first CTO, writes Larder Core.

  2. Jun 2024

    The first dark store, Noord, opens: about 300 orders a day.

  3. Nov 2024

    The second store, Zuid, opens after five months of code changes: everything assumed one store.

  4. Apr 2025

    King's Day: checkout times out for 40 minutes at five times the normal peak.

  5. Jul 2025

    4,000 orders a day, and 6 % of them end in a refund ticket, mostly over substitutions.

  6. Aug 2025

    Jasper leaves. Nobody else knows why Larder Core looks the way it does.

  7. Sep 2025

    The Series B closes, on one condition: six cities by the end of 2027.

  8. Oct 2025

    Ana joins as the first solution architect, and Grace as product owner. Series 2 starts here.

4The goals six cities by the end of 2027

Femke, Marco and Joost agreed four business drivers in October 2025. Every book measures its choices against them:

  • Open cities fast: a new city in 8 weeks or less (Zuid took 20)
  • Keep the promise: 95 % of express orders on time (today 88 %)
  • Unit economics: cost per order down to €2.80 (today €4.10)
  • Stay trusted: no lost orders, no double charges, GDPR-clean, ready for Belgium

And a second business: Larder for Business

Femke wants a second revenue line with predictable volume: Larder for Business, weekly restocking of office pantries (fruit, snacks, drinks, coffee), delivered by van between 07:00 and 09:00 and invoiced monthly.

The first client is Korenwolf Group (fictional), an insurance and pensions company with about 2,400 staff in Amsterdam, Rotterdam and Utrecht. Hanna, Head of Workplace, wants happy staff. Ruben, from procurement, wants a fair price and firm service levels, and compares everything with the old caterer.

met at a trade fair in January 2026 → pilot at the Amsterdam office in May → a three-office contract in September (98 % complete deliveries, by 09:00, a monthly report) → the first hard renewal talk in 2027. Big, scheduled, invoiced orders stretch Grace's ordering flow, Ana's architecture and Sanne's Rotterdam plan.

6The books one company, four notebooks

  • Book 1 · narrated by Ana · Oct 2025 →vols. 1–2 out

    The Solution Architect's Notebook

    How Ana turns a one-line brief into an architecture that can grow to six cities: understanding the problem, finding the requirements that matter, choosing first shapes, testing risky ideas early, then recording decisions and living with them.

  • Book 2 · narrated by Grace · Oct 2025 →vols. 1–2 out

    The Product Owner's Notebook

    The same months through Grace's eyes: what customers really need, why substitutions cost 6 % of orders, which bets to make, how to say no to the loudest idea, and how to know if anything worked. Many scenes are the same meetings Ana is in.

  • Book 3 · narrated by Sanne · Jan 2026 →planned

    The Project Manager's Notebook

    Sanne runs the Rotterdam launch: a fixed date, a signed lease, three teams, two vendors and one risky integration. Planning, estimates, dependencies, risks and status reports that tell the truth.

  • Book 4 · narrated by Mo · Mar 2026 →planned

    The Client Manager's Notebook

    Larder for Business wins its first client, Korenwolf Group. Mo learns to manage expectations, scope changes, service levels, monthly reports and a hard renewal, with Hanna and Ruben on the other side of the table.
