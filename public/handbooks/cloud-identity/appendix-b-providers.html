B.5Provider-neutral standards

These are the same everywhere, which is why Part I comes first: OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect (Chapter 4), SAML 2.0 (Chapter 5), SCIM 2.0 (Chapter 13), WebAuthn and passkeys (Chapter 2), TOTP (Chapter 2), JWT (Chapter 3) and mutual TLS (Chapter 19). Every provider's identity services are built from them, so knowing the standard lets you read any provider's documentation.
