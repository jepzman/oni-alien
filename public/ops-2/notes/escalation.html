Who's Next If Nobody Answers?

TL;DR

An escalation policy says who is paged next, and after how long, when the first person doesn't answer: primary → secondary after 5 minutes → a named escalation after 15, by phone call. Alongside it, keep a short list of specialists the incident lead can pull in. And test the chain: phones change, updates switch on silent modes, numbers go stale. A single page into a silent phone shouldn't mean nobody comes.

1The situation Larder, Sunday 2 August 2026

At 07:20 on a Sunday, just as the store opens, Rotterdam's store heartbeat goes silent. The page goes to Ana, who is on call. Overnight, her phone had installed an update that switched on a do-not-disturb mode and silenced the paging app. No one else is paged: the paging tool has no escalation set. For 22 minutes nobody is on it, until Yara phones Eli's personal number at 07:42.

  • One silent phone means no response at all.
  • The secondary exists on paper, but the tool doesn't know about them.
  • In the end the front line works around the process, by calling Eli.

2Real-life analogy the school's sick-child list

When a child is sick, the school has a list on file: call mum; if there's no answer in five minutes, call dad; then grandma. The school doesn't guess who might be free, and it doesn't wait an hour for mum to call back. The order and the times were decided before anyone was sick.

3How it solves it a chain with timeouts, a specialist list, and a test page

On Monday 3 August the escalation policy goes into the paging tool:

  • A page goes to the primary.
  • If it isn't acknowledged in 5 minutes, it pages the secondary as well.
  • After 15 minutes it pages Eli as escalation and phones him, because a phone call gets through settings that a push notification doesn't.

Separately, the incident lead has a specialist list to pull people in on purpose: payments → Cho, mobile and handhelds → Dee, database → Eli, a store → its lead on duty, vendors → ops/vendors.md.

The last change is the cheapest: on their first on-call morning, each primary sends themselves a test page and checks it rings. Ana's phone setting is in the review as what it was, a phone setting and not a person failing, and the test page catches it in two more phones in August.

  1. Set an acknowledgement timeout

    5 minutes is common for paging alerts.

  2. Escalate to the secondary, then a named person

    Each step in the tool, not just on paper.

  3. Use a phone call at the last step

    It gets through more silent modes than a push notification.

  4. Keep a short specialist list

    For the lead to pull people in on purpose.

  5. Test the chain every rota week

    A test page on the first morning.

  6. Review every missed page without blame

    It's usually a setting, not a person.

Vocabulary for the exam

Acknowledge (ack)
Telling the paging tool “I've got it”, which stops escalation.
Acknowledgement timeout
How long before the page goes to the next person.
Escalation policy
The ordered chain of who is paged, with timeouts.
Functional escalation
Pulling in someone with the right skills (a specialist).
Hierarchical escalation
Telling someone more senior because of impact or risk.
Test page
A deliberate page to check that phone and settings work.

6Common pitfalls the usual ways this goes wrong

  • The chain only on paper

    The secondary is never actually paged. Fix → set it in the tool.

  • Push notifications only

    Silent modes swallow them. Fix → phone call at the last step.

  • Blaming the person

    Ana gets the blame for a phone update. Fix → review the setting, test everyone's phone.

  • Specialists by guesswork

    The lead calls whoever answered last time. Fix → a short named list.

7When to use it (and when not)

Use it when…

  • Pages have gone unanswered.
  • The front line calls people's personal numbers.
  • A new person joins the rota.
  • After a phone or tool change.

Skip it when…

  • Never, if anything pages at night.

8Self-check quiz

What does an acknowledgement do?

Tells the tool someone has it, which stops further escalation.

What is Larder's chain?

Primary; after 5 minutes also the secondary; after 15 also Eli, by page and phone call.

Why a phone call at the last step?

It gets through silent modes and notification settings that push notifications don't.

What's the difference between functional and hierarchical escalation?

Functional pulls in skills (a specialist); hierarchical informs someone senior because of impact.

What did the review conclude about Ana?

Nothing about Ana: a phone update changed a setting. The fix was the chain and a weekly test page.

what would you do?Your test page doesn't ring on Monday morning. What do you do?

Fix the phone settings now, send another test, and tell the secondary in the handover in case it happens again. It's much better to find out on Monday at 10:00 than on Sunday at 07:20.

try it

Look at your paging setup: if the first person doesn't answer, who is paged next, and after how long? Send yourself a test page today. Does it ring with your phone's current settings?

remember!!

A page into one silent phone is a page to nobody. Set a chain with timeouts, and test it weekly.
