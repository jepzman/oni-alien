Growing the Product

Volume 6 of The Product Owner's Notebook, the last: how a product owner grows a product, and themselves. 15 notes in four collections: new markets, business customers, scaling the product, and growing yourself. Every note follows Grace at Larder from April to December 2027, in the same months as Ana's fourth and fifth notebooks, and ends with a try it exercise for your own product.

the story so far

April 2027. Utrecht opens, Antwerp is coming, Larder Plus is about to launch and business customers want more than standing orders. Grace's product has grown from one problem into several products, and she has grown with it. This volume follows her from April to December 2027: launching cities and a country, serving business and consumer customers without splitting the product, scaling it with principles and platforms, and finally hiring the product owner who takes part of it over.

★The nine product ratings what a good product practice is judged on

Every note scores the pattern from 1 (poor) to 5 (great) on the same nine things, comparing an app without the pattern to the same app with it.

Customer value
Customers would notice it, and miss it if it went.
Business impact
It shows up in a number the business already tracks.
Evidence
Decided on what we learned, not on opinion or rank.
Learning speed
How soon we know if we're wrong.
Focus
The team works on a few things at once, and knows which matter most.
Shared understanding
Everyone can explain what we're building, for whom and why.
Stakeholder trust
Leaders and partners trust the plan and the updates.
Flow
Value reaches customers in small, frequent steps.
Low overhead
Time and ceremony it takes. High = light.
