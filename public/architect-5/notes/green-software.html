Green Software

TL;DR

Green software reduces the carbon emissions of running software. Start by measuring (the cloud provider's carbon report, plus estimates), express it per unit of business (per order), then use less (idle resources, data you don't need, heavy transfers), shift flexible work to times and places with cleaner electricity, and choose cleaner regions. Report the number with its method, and be honest about where the bigger emissions are.

  • Family: cost & balance
  • Origin: Green Software Foundation (2021), Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) specification
  • Lives in: a yearly carbon page and the cost dashboard
  • Pairs with: cost-aware architecture, data classification (retention), capacity planning

1The situation Larder, 6 March 2028

Korenwolf reports its own emissions and sends suppliers a questionnaire: what are the emissions of the services you provide us? Mo forwards it to Ana: what do we answer for the platform? Nobody at Larder has ever measured it.

  • Clients increasingly ask suppliers for emissions data.
  • Without a number, there's nothing to improve or report.
  • Guesses either overclaim or undersell.

2Real-life analogy a greener home

To run a home more greenly you first read the meter. Then you use less (switch off what's idle), run the washing machine when electricity is cleanest, and choose a greener supplier. Without the meter, every step is a guess.

Translating it to the job
In real lifeOn the project

3How it solves it measure, use less, shift, choose

Ana and Eli measured: the provider's carbon report plus estimates for data transfer, about 9 tonnes CO₂e a year, about 0.9 g per order. They used less (the cost cuts had removed idle resources; event archives cut from 40 to 12 TB; image compression cut app data by 38 %), shifted nightly reporting and backup copies to cleaner hours, and chose the backup region partly on carbon intensity. Result: about 0.6 g per order. The answer to Korenwolf gave the number, the method, the plan, and said plainly that the e-bikes and vans matter far more.

  1. Measure

    Provider carbon tools; estimate what they miss.

  2. Express per unit

    Grams of CO₂e per order (like SCI).

  3. Use less

    Idle resources, unneeded data, heavy transfers.

  4. Shift flexible work

    To cleaner hours or regions.

  5. Choose cleaner places

    When picking regions and vendors.

  6. Report with the method

    And name the bigger sources honestly.

Vocabulary for the exam

CO₂e
Carbon dioxide equivalent: all greenhouse gases in one unit.
Carbon intensity
Grams of CO₂e per kilowatt-hour of electricity.
SCI
Software Carbon Intensity: emissions per functional unit.
Carbon-aware
Running flexible work when or where electricity is cleaner.
Embodied carbon
Emissions from making the hardware.
Scope 3
Emissions in a company's supply chain, e.g. its suppliers' services.

Done (Feb–Mar 2028)
  idle test environments off nights/weekends · event archives 40 TB → 12 TB (retention per class)

Now          ≈ 0.6 g CO₂e per order (SCI-style: per order, per year) · next review March 2029

tools: the cloud provider's carbon footprint tool, the Green Software Foundation's SCI specification, carbon intensity data (e.g. Electricity Maps), the cost dashboard.

4Gains vs losses nothing is free

What we gain

  • An honest answer for clients.
  • Overlaps with cost cuts: less waste.
  • Per-order number to improve.
  • Method in the open.
  • Bigger sources named, not hidden.

What we lose

  • Measurements are estimates.
  • Provider tools differ and lag.
  • Small next to deliveries.
  • Shifting work adds scheduling complexity.

6Common pitfalls the usual ways this goes wrong

  • Overclaiming

    “Carbon-neutral platform.” Fix → the number, the method, the limits.

  • Ignoring the big sources

    Platform optimised; vans ignored. Fix → say where the real emissions are.

  • No unit

    Tonnes rise as you grow. Fix → grams per order.

  • Green at any cost

    Reliability cut to save grams. Fix → balance with other qualities.

7When to use it (and when not)

Use it when…

  • Client or investor questionnaires.
  • Choosing regions and vendors.
  • Alongside cost reviews.
  • Retention and archiving decisions.

Skip it when…

  • Never entirely, but keep it proportionate to the platform's share.

8Self-check quiz

What are the four steps?

Measure, use less, shift flexible work, choose cleaner places.

Why express emissions per order?

Totals rise with growth; per order shows real improvement.

What was Larder's platform figure?

About 0.9 g CO₂e per order, cut to about 0.6 g.

What did Ana say honestly?

That deliveries by e-bike and van matter far more than the platform.
