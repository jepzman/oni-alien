Building the Reliability Team

Shaping the team who runs what, who joins, and an on-call people can live with

  1. 01Who Runs What?Choose per service: the team that builds it runs it, or a reliability team does, with clear terms either way.p. 01
  2. 02Hiring the First SREsHire for curiosity, calm and coding, with a work sample that looks like the real job.p. 02
  3. 03On-Call People Can Live WithMeasure on-call load, cap it, pay and rest fairly, and fix the pages instead of toughening the people.p. 03

Handing over readiness, the pager, and the weeks after

  1. 04Is This Service Ready for Production?A production readiness review before a team takes on a service: SLOs, alerts, runbooks, capacity, a way back.p. 04
  2. 05Handing Over the PagerMove on-call responsibility in steps: shadow, reverse shadow, game days, then a date, with a way back.p. 05
  3. 06Did the Handover Work?Early life support: a few weeks of extra help and close watching after a handover, with an exit test.p. 06

Setting direction what to automate, which tools, where we stand and where we're going

  1. 07What to Automate NextChoose automation by toil saved and risk removed, keep a toil budget, and automate the whole job, not half.p. 07
  2. 08Which Tools Do We Really Need?Fewer tools, each with an owner and a job; consolidate overlaps and check what each one costs and saves.p. 08
  3. 09Where Are We Weak?A short, honest maturity check per practice, done together, to choose what to improve next.p. 09
  4. 10Where Are We Going?An operations strategy on a page and a roadmap tied to the business plan.p. 10

Leading improvement that lasts, change, AI, careers and making it yours

  1. 11Continual Improvement That ContinuesOne improvement register for everything, a small budget of time, and a rhythm that outlives the enthusiasm.p. 11
  2. 12Leading Change in How Teams WorkChange how teams work with a reason, a pilot, help, and patience; people adopt what helps them.p. 12
  3. 13AI in Operations: Where It Helps, Where It LiesUse AI for drafts, summaries and search, never as the last word on production; check every fact.p. 13
  4. 14Growing Operations EngineersA career path, coaching and real responsibility, so people grow into the jobs you used to do.p. 14
  5. 15Making It YoursLook back at the whole book, pick what fits your place, and start where you are.p. 15
