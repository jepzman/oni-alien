Handing Over the Pager

TL;DR

Don't move on-call for a critical service in one swap. Move it in steps. Shadow: the new team gets every page and watches. Reverse shadow: the new team is primary, the old team backs them up. Game days practise the hard incidents. Then a date, with the old team as second line and a way back written in advance. Nobody should take their first real page for a critical service alone.

1The situation Larder, August 2028

The simplest plan for moving Ordering's on-call: on 1 October, swap the schedule in the paging tool. Ben's team stops being paged, and the reliability team starts. But Lotte joined in July and Yusuf in August, and neither has ever handled a real Ordering incident. Ben is worried. So is Femke, who remembers Christmas 2026.

  • A big-bang swap puts the least experienced people on the most critical service.
  • Knowledge moves by doing, not by reading.
  • If it goes wrong, nobody has agreed how to step back.

2Real-life analogy taking over a school bus route

A new driver taking over a school bus route first rides along and watches. Then they drive while the old driver sits beside them. Then they practise the hard bits in the depot: a breakdown, a missing child. Only then do they drive alone, with the old driver's phone number in their pocket.

3How it solves it shadow, reverse shadow, game days, a date, a way back

Eli and Ben plan the handover in steps through September:

  • Shadow, 4–17 September: the reliability team gets every Ordering page alongside Ben's team. They watch, ask and update runbooks.
  • Reverse shadow, 18–30 September: the reliability team is primary. Ben's team is secondary and steps in only if asked.
  • Game days, Thursdays 14 and 21 September: a failed deploy, the database failover, the address lookup vendor down, and a major-incident drill. Lotte and Yusuf lead, and Eli only watches.
  • 1 October: the reliability team is primary for Ordering, and Ben's team is second line.
  • Way back, written in advance: if there are two SEV1s, or the error budget is spent in October, Ben's team returns as primary while gaps are fixed.

By 1 October everyone on the reliability rota has handled at least three real Ordering pages with someone beside them. Nobody takes their first real page alone.

Vocabulary for the exam

Shadowing
Receiving pages and watching, without being responsible.
Reverse shadowing
Being responsible, with an experienced person backing you up.
Transition
Moving a service, or responsibility for it, from one state or team to another.
Second line
The team called for deeper knowledge after the first responder.
Way back
The agreed conditions for returning responsibility.
Game day
A planned exercise of failures and responses (vol. 5).

6Common pitfalls the usual ways this goes wrong

  • A big-bang swap

    First page alone, on the most critical service. Fix → steps.

  • Shadowing without asking

    Watching without learning. Fix → ask and update runbooks during shadow.

  • Game days led by the experts

    The new people only watch. Fix → the newest lead.

  • No way back

    A bad October becomes a crisis. Fix → write the conditions first.

7When to use it (and when not)

Use it when…

  • Moving on-call for a service between teams.
  • New people joining a critical rota.
  • Before taking over a vendor's or another team's service.
  • After a readiness review.

Skip it when…

  • Low-tier services: a handover meeting and a runbook review may do.

8Self-check quiz

What are the steps of Larder's pager handover?

Shadow, reverse shadow, game days, a handover date, and a way back.

What's the difference between shadow and reverse shadow?

In shadow, the new team watches; in reverse shadow, it's primary with the old team backing up.

Who led the game days?

Lotte and Yusuf, the newest people; Eli only watched.

What's Ben's team's role after 1 October?

Second line.

What's the way back?

Two SEV1s or the error budget spent in October → Ben's team primary again while gaps are fixed.

what would you do?During reverse shadow Yusuf is paged at 02:00 and doesn't call Ben's secondary, though he's unsure. It goes fine. What do you say in the morning?

Well done for handling it; and next time, call: that's what reverse shadow is for. Unsure at 02:00 is exactly when to use the backup, and it costs nothing.
