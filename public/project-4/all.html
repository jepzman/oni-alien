Money and People

The Finance and Resources domains: cost estimates, the budget and its reserves, cash flow, committed vs spent, earned value, forecasting, payback, RACI, estimating and getting people, levelling, leading without authority, conflict and keeping resources available.

Planning the money what it costs, what is set aside, and when the cash goes out

  1. 01Whose Money Is It?Agree, before spending, what the budget covers, who may spend what, and how money is counted and reported.p. 01
  2. 02What Will It Cost?Estimate each piece of work in money, from quotes, rates and past jobs, and say how sure each number is.p. 02
  3. 03How Much Do We Set Aside? + goes deeperAdd the estimates into a budget, put a visible reserve on top, and spread it over time.p. 03
  4. 04When Does the Money Leave?Spending is agreed when it's committed but leaves the bank later. Plan the cash, not just the total.p. 04

Watching the money is the money buying progress, where will it end, and was it worth it?

  1. 05How Much Have We Spent?Three different numbers: committed, actually spent, and forecast. Report all three, line by line.p. 05
  2. 06Is the Money Buying Progress? + goes deeperCompare the value of the work done with what was planned and what it cost, in one set of numbers.p. 06
  3. 07Where Will the Money End Up?Forecast the final cost two ways, by formula and line by line, and compare them with the budget.p. 07
  4. 08Was It Worth It?Put the money spent next to the money the result brings back, and say when it pays for itself.p. 08

Getting the people and things who does what, how many, and where they come from

  1. 09Who Does What?A responsibility table: who does the work, who answers for it, who is asked and who is told.p. 09
  2. 10How Many People, and What Else?Work out the people and equipment from the demand they must serve, not from habit.p. 10
  3. 11Where Do They Come From?Hire, borrow, lease or buy, and agree every loan with whoever owns the person or thing.p. 11

Leading and balancing keep people from overload, pulling together, and available

  1. 12Who Is Overloaded? + goes deeperSpot people booked over 100 % and move work in time or to others before they break.p. 12
  2. 13How Do I Lead People Who Don't Report to Me? + goes deeperLead with a shared goal, facts, favours and credit when you can't give orders.p. 13
  3. 14Why Is the Team Arguing?Teams go through stages and conflict is part of them. Work on the interests behind the positions.p. 14
  4. 15Are the People and Things Still There?Check every day that the people, equipment and suppliers the plan needs are actually available.p. 15
