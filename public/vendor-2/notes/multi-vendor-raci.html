Who Does What When Three Firms Share a Project?

TL;DR

When several firms share a system, failures fall into the gaps between them. For every shared interface, write a responsibility chart with all the firms in the room: what has to happen, and for each row exactly one accountable owner. Include the unhappy rows (“the file fails at 02:10”), agree joint triage by evidence, and get the client to sign it, because only the client has a contract with everyone.

  • Family: two firms, one team
  • Lives in: delivery/lodestar/IA-01-raci.md (interface agreement IA-01)
  • Pairs with: the incumbent, governance

1The situation The Grid, 21 July 2027

Every night at 02:10, MAVIS sends a file to Halvorsen billing. Once wave 1 starts, migrated customers will come from Wrenfield's new extract instead. Three firms will touch one file: MetroVolt IT runs the batch scheduler, Halvorsen ingests the file, Wrenfield builds the new extract.

  • Bert tells Ines that in 2024 the file failed three nights running. Each team spent the mornings proving it wasn't theirs. Customers got wrong bills for a month.
  • Sven's one request at the café in March: don't blame Halvorsen for MAVIS's data.
  • The contract says who Wrenfield works for. It says nothing about who answers the phone at 02:15.

2Both sides of the table the same moment, seen twice

MetroVolt Hollis and MetroVolt IT

MetroVolt has contracts with each firm, and no contract between them. When something fails between suppliers, MetroVolt is the only party who can make them work together, and it's tired of refereeing blame.

“I don't care whose fault it is. I care who picks up the phone.”

Wrenfield Ines, with Sven

Wrenfield is new and the obvious suspect for any failure near its code. It needs a written, shared answer to “who owns what” before the first failure, signed by MetroVolt so it binds Halvorsen too.

“Write the 02:10 night down now, while we're all friends.”

A blame war is just a responsibility chart written after the failure, by angry people.

3Real-life analogy a relay race handover

In a relay, races are lost at the handover, not on the straights. Teams practise the exchange zone: who reaches, who grabs, what the signal is, and what happens if the baton drops. Everyone knows who picks it up. Nobody stands over a dropped baton arguing about whose hand it fell from.

4How it works one chart, all firms in the room, one A per row

On Wednesday 21 July, Ines runs a two-hour workshop with Sven and a Halvorsen engineer, Hollis's integration lead and the MetroVolt IT on-call manager, and Hana. One whiteboard, one question per row: what has to happen, and who is accountable?

They list the happy rows (file format, the new extract, the schedule, ingestion) and then the unhappy rows: the file is late, the file is malformed, the totals don't match. Each row gets exactly one A. Arguing about which firm gets the A is the useful part: it takes forty minutes for “a failed file at 02:10”. The answer: MetroVolt IT on-call is accountable for the first response (it runs the scheduler and is awake), and starts a joint triage with the others, by evidence, within four hours.

Sven gets what he asked for in March: interface defects are assigned after evidence, not before. The chart becomes interface agreement IA-01, signed by all three firms and by Hollis for MetroVolt on 11 August, and logged as DL-005.

  1. List every shared interface

    Files, APIs, schedules, data handovers.

  2. Get every firm in the room

    Engineers, not just account managers.

  3. Write the happy rows, then the unhappy ones

    Late, malformed, mismatched, silent.

  4. Exactly one A per row

    Argue until there is one.

  5. Agree joint triage by evidence

    Who starts it, how fast, what evidence.

  6. Get the client to sign it

    Only the client has a contract with everyone.

Vocabulary

RACI
Responsible, accountable, consulted, informed: who does what for each task.
Accountable
The one person or team who answers for a task being done.
Interface agreement
A written agreement on how two systems and their owners connect.
Joint triage
Investigating a cross-supplier problem together, by evidence.
Service integration
Coordinating several suppliers as one service (often called SIAM).
Hand-off
The moment work or data passes from one owner to another.

5What the contract says how a third party's role becomes binding

Lodestar SOW · §3.6 and IA-0111 Aug 2027
countersigned

3.6 Where a Dependency is to be provided by a third-party supplier of MetroVolt, MetroVolt shall use reasonable endeavours to procure its provision.

MetroVolt signing IA-01 is how it “procures” Halvorsen's part.

IA-01, 4 Defects at the interface are assigned to a party only after joint triage on the evidence listed in section 3.

Sven's sentence. it protects us just as much.

In plain words: you can't contract with the other vendor, but the client can. A responsibility chart the client countersigns turns a friendly workshop into something every firm can be held to.

9Common pitfalls the usual ways this goes wrong

  • Happy rows only

    The chart is silent when it matters. Fix → write the failure rows.

  • Two As

    Shared accountability means none. Fix → argue until there's one.

  • Account managers only

    The people at 02:15 weren't asked. Fix → engineers and on-call in the room.

  • Blame before evidence

    Each firm defends itself. Fix → joint triage by agreed evidence.

  • Not signed by the client

    Nothing binds the other vendor. Fix → the client countersigns.

10When to use it (and when not)

Use it when…

  • Several suppliers share a system or interface.
  • An incumbent and a newcomer meet at a boundary.
  • Past failures were spent assigning blame.

Skip it when…

  • A single supplier owns everything end to end.

11Self-check quiz

Why does each row need exactly one A?

Shared accountability means nobody answers when it fails.

What are “unhappy rows”?

The failure cases: late, malformed or mismatched files, silence.

Who is accountable for a failed file at 02:10, and why?

MetroVolt IT on-call: it runs the scheduler and is awake; it starts joint triage.

What did Sven get from IA-01?

Defects assigned after evidence, not before.

Why must MetroVolt sign?

Only MetroVolt has a contract with every firm.

what would you do?Two months later, Halvorsen changes the file format with a week's notice. What do you do?

Point to IA-01: format changes need 30 days' notice. Raise it with Hollis, log it, and assess the impact; don't absorb it quietly or argue directly with Sven first.

try it

Pick one interface between your team and another team or firm. Write the rows, happy and unhappy, and put one A on each. Who would you need in the room to agree it?

remember!!

Blame wars happen in the gaps. For every shared interface, one A per row, unhappy rows included, signed by the client.
