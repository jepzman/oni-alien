Discovering the Problem

Customer interviews, jobs to be done, opportunity trees, the right metrics and cheap tests: finding the problem worth solving.

Understanding customers listen before you decide anything

  1. 01Customer InterviewsAsk customers about what they did last time, not what they'd like, and listen for the pain.p. 01
  2. 02Jobs to Be DoneFind the progress customers are trying to make; the product is just something they hire for the job.p. 02
  3. 03Proto-PersonasQuick, evidence-based sketches of the main kinds of customer, kept honest and kept few.p. 03
  4. 04Customer Journey MapDraw every step a customer takes, what they feel at each one, and where it breaks.p. 04

Framing the problem pick the problem worth solving, and say it clearly

  1. 05Problem StatementsWrite the problem in one sentence, from the customer's side, before anyone proposes a fix.p. 05
  2. 06Opportunity Solution TreeLink one outcome to the customer needs behind it, then to ideas, then to tests.p. 06
  3. 07Assumption MappingList what must be true for an idea to work, and test the riskiest first.p. 07
  4. 08Outcomes over OutputsJudge work by the change it makes for customers, not by the features shipped.p. 08

Measuring what matters know what “better” means before you build

  1. 09North Star MetricOne number that captures the value customers get, so every team pulls the same way.p. 09
  2. 10Leading vs Lagging IndicatorsPair the slow result you care about with fast signals you can move this week.p. 10
  3. 11Baseline Before You BuildMeasure today properly first, or you'll never know if the change worked.p. 11

Testing ideas cheaply find out if you're wrong in days, not months

  1. 12Testable HypothesesWrite each idea as a bet: we believe, so we'll, and we'll know when.p. 12
  2. 13Fake Door TestShow the button before building the feature, and count who knocks.p. 13
  3. 14Concierge & Wizard-of-Oz MVPDeliver the value by hand first; build the software only once it's proven.p. 14
  4. 15Prototype Testing with UsersPut a clickable fake in front of five real users and watch where they stumble.p. 15
