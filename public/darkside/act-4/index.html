The Darkside of the Corporation · Act IV

The Ascension

The stairway to the next level is not where they told you it was

Everyone at MetroVolt is told the same story about promotions: do great work, and you'll be noticed. It's a lovely story. It's about a third true.

The rest is invisible: who speaks for you in rooms you're not in, what you wrote down, how you're described in a meeting called calibration, whether anyone knows what you actually did, and whether you asked. This Act is about that invisible two-thirds.

— the AnnotatorGreat work is the entry ticket, not the prize. The prize goes to great work that someone can describe in one sentence, in a room you're not in.

It's autumn 2027. Horizon is over. Priya Nair has been promoted to Director of Customer, and she has created a new role beneath her: Head of Customer Insight, leading nine people, Pip's analysts and Dorian's planners together. Pip wants it. So does Dorian. So, it turns out, does someone from outside. And Pip is about to discover whether he wants what he wants.

How to read an entry

  1. A sighting: the creature in the wild, at MetroVolt Electric Corporation, scene by scene.
  2. Origin: how it came to be. Most monsters were bitten once, and the company rewarded what happened next.
  3. Know thy enemy: what it wants, why it thrives, what it fears, and its three breeds.
  4. Signs and omens, counter-spells (with the exact words: the incantation) and the forbidden spells you must not cast.
  5. Difficulty (Normal, Nightmare, Hell): how to tell which you're facing, and what to do at each.
  6. Are you the creature? A mirror. Then the rehearsal (the counter-spells, said out loud) and the trial.
