The Darkside of the Corporation

A Survival Grimoire for the Cursed Employed

This book was found in a supply cupboard on Floor 13 of The Grid, the 31-storey glass tower of MetroVolt Electric Corporation (“Powering Tomorrow, Today™”). Floor 13 does not officially exist. Neither, officially, do the things in this book.

It is written by the Chronicler, who has survived nineteen years, seven reorgs, four CEOs and one “transformation journey”. The margins belong to the Annotator, who left. Read it as a field guide to the parts of working life no onboarding deck mentions: politics, difficult people, bad managers, layoffs, promotions, rival vendors and impossible clients. The jokes are the wrapping paper. The advice inside is real.

— the AnnotatorEvery counter-spell in here worked for somebody. The forbidden spells also worked, for about a day.
