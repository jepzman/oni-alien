The Darkside of the Corporation · Act VI

The Outer Realms

Beyond the castle walls there are worse things than coworkers

Inside MetroVolt you can, at least, look your enemies in the eye at the coffee machine. Outside the walls, the creatures have purchase orders.

A client can't be reorganised, a vendor can't be sent on a course, and a competitor won't come to your leaving drinks. They have their own bosses, budgets and fears, and they will make all of them your problem. This Act is about the realms beyond the castle: the people who pay you, the people you pay, and the people who want both.

— the AnnotatorEvery external relationship is two organisations pretending to be two people. Find out who's really on the other side of the table.

It's July 2028. MetroVolt has launched GridSight, a small data-services arm that sells grid and demand forecasts to councils, water companies and developers. Ruth Calloway sponsors it. Felix Marr sells it. Pip, now Principal Analyst, has been seconded as its delivery lead, with Asha, a laptop each, and a first client who rings at ten at night.

How to read an entry

  1. A sighting: the creature in the wild, at MetroVolt Electric Corporation, scene by scene.
  2. Origin: how it came to be. Most monsters were bitten once, and the company rewarded what happened next.
  3. Know thy enemy: what it wants, why it thrives, what it fears, and its three breeds.
  4. Signs and omens, counter-spells (with the exact words: the incantation) and the forbidden spells you must not cast.
  5. Difficulty (Normal, Nightmare, Hell): how to tell which you're facing, and what to do at each.
  6. Are you the creature? A mirror. Then the rehearsal (the counter-spells, said out loud) and the trial.
