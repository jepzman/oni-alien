The Weather Tyrant

Sunshine on Monday. Thunder by Tuesday. No forecast.

Threat
Habitat
Monday mornings, budget weeks, the minutes after a call with his boss
Weakness
patterns, written down, and a calm that doesn't move with the weather

IA sighting

Seconded to Network Assets for the meter fix, Pip learned to read Gareth Pound like a barometer. On good days Gareth was funny and generous and bought pastries. On bad days he slammed his laptop shut, said “unacceptable” to slides he'd approved the week before, and once made a junior engineer cry over a font.

The team had a private chat called ☀️ / ⛈️. Someone posted the forecast each morning after seeing him in the lift. Nobody brought bad news on a ⛈️ day. Bad news therefore arrived in large, late piles.

IIKnow thy enemy

Moody managers are usually stressed and poorly regulated: they bring pressure from above straight into the room. Their moods have patterns (days, deadlines, meetings with their boss), even if they can't see them themselves.

You are not responsible for their weather. But you can forecast it, time your asks, and keep your own behaviour steady. And there is a line: shouting, humiliation or fear is not weather. It's conduct, and it has a channel.

— the AnnotatorYou can work for a moody manager for a while. You can't work for a cruel one. Learn the difference early: moods change; cruelty repeats.

IIISigns and omens

Tick two or more and you are in its lair.

  • The team checks “what mood is he in” before asking anything.
  • Approved work gets rejected on a bad day.
  • Bad news is held back and arrives late.
  • You feel relief when they're out of the office.

IVCounter-spells

  1. 1The Barometer

    Notice the pattern (days, times, after which meetings). Time important asks for good windows.

  2. 2The Steady Hand

    Stay calm and consistent whatever the weather. Don't absorb the mood or reflect it back.

    incantation“I can see today's tough. Shall I come back with this at four, or do you want the two-minute version now?”

  3. 3The Written Approval

    Get approvals in writing on good days, so bad days can't quietly undo them.

    incantation“Great, so we're agreed on the layout. I'll note it in the project log.”

  4. 4The Line in the Sand

    If moods turn into shouting, humiliation or fear, name it calmly, privately, and take it to HR if it repeats.

    incantation“I want to do good work for you, but I'm not OK being shouted at. Can we talk about what went wrong?”

VForbidden spells

Tempting. Effective for about a day. Each one has a soul cost = one ember of your soul.

  • Storm Chasing

    Bringing everything on good days only. Urgent bad news on bad days is still urgent; delay makes it worse.

  • Becoming the Weather

    Matching his mood: snapping back, sulking. Now there are two storm systems.

  • The Forecast Channel

    The ☀️ / ⛈️ chat is funny. Until it's screenshotted. Then it's a disciplinary.

VIDifficulty

The same creature at three strengths. Pick the one you're facing.

Normal

Occasional bad days under pressure. Time your asks, stay steady, get things in writing.

Nightmare

Frequent swings that affect decisions. Written approvals, and a quiet word with them on a good day about the impact.

Hell

Shouting, humiliation, people in tears. That's not a mood, it's misconduct. Keep a dated record, support colleagues, go to HR or your skip-level.

IXThe trial

What would you do? Tap a question for the answer.

Your manager approved a plan yesterday and rejects it today, angrily. What do you do?

Stay calm, refer to yesterday's written approval, and ask what's changed: “Yesterday we agreed X. Has something changed I should know about?”

You have urgent bad news and it's a ⛈️ day. Wait?

No. Deliver it short and calm, with a plan. Delayed bad news is worse news.

Your manager shouts at a colleague in front of the team. What now?

Support the colleague afterwards, note it with date and facts, and encourage them (or yourself) to raise it. That's conduct, not weather.

The Annotator's last wordYou can't control the weather. You can control whether you bring an umbrella, and a written record.
