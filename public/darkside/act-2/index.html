The Bestiary

Know the creatures of the open plan

Every office has a bestiary. Ours lives on floors 4 to 27 of The Grid, headquarters of MetroVolt Electric Corporation: 6,200 souls who keep the lights on for 2.3 million homes and cannot, between them, book a meeting room.

The creatures in this Act are not villains. Most are ordinary people who have learned that a certain bad behaviour gets rewarded. That is good news: behaviour that was learned can be blocked, starved or redirected. You cannot change the creature. You can change what it gets from you.

— the AnnotatorRule zero: if a creature is harassing, threatening or discriminating, it is not a creature. It is a case. Write it down and go to HR or a lawyer. Spells are for annoying people, not dangerous ones.

Our guide through the Act is Pip, a junior analyst in Grid Planning, level 3, full of hope. Watch what happens to his hope. Learn from it.

How to read an entry

  1. A sighting: the creature in the wild, at MetroVolt Electric Corporation.
  2. Know thy enemy: why it behaves like this. Most monsters are just scared people with calendars.
  3. Signs and omens, counter-spells (with the exact words: the incantation) and the forbidden spells you must not cast.
  4. Difficulty (Normal, Nightmare, Hell), survival stats, a loot drop to copy, and the trial.
