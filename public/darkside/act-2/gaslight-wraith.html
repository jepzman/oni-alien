The Gaslight Wraith

“I never said that.” (It did. You were there.)

Threat
Habitat
verbal agreements, hallway chats, calls with no notes
Weakness
the written recap, sent within the hour

IA sighting

On the 9th of April, in the lift, Rhona told Pip to “pause the substation analysis and put everything on Lumen meter data.” Pip did. On the 23rd, in the Lumen review, Rhona asked in front of nine people why the substation analysis was late. “I never said to pause it. I'd never say that.”

Pip opened his mouth and found nothing in it but the memory of a lift, and a lift is not evidence.

IIKnow thy enemy

Some Wraiths do it on purpose, to dodge blame. Most don't: they change their mind, forget, and then sincerely remember the version where they were right. Memory is not a recording; it is rewritten every time we recall it, usually in our own favour.

It does not matter which kind you face. The cure is the same: a shared written record, made at the time, that both of you saw. The Wraith is a creature of fog. Paper does not fog.

— the AnnotatorIf someone repeatedly denies things you both know happened and it is making you doubt your own memory, that is not a quirk. Keep notes, tell someone you trust, and read the Hell difficulty below.

IIISigns and omens

Tick two or more and you are in its lair.

  • Big decisions are made in lifts, corridors and “quick calls”.
  • They never put instructions in writing.
  • “That's not what I meant” follows every problem.
  • You start recording things in your head “just in case”.
  • Colleagues tell you, quietly, “oh, she does that”.

IVCounter-spells

  1. 1The Binding Recap

    After any spoken instruction, send a short written recap within the hour. Make it friendly and ask them to correct it. Silence becomes agreement.

    incantation“Just to check I've got it right from our chat: pause the substation analysis, all hands on Lumen meter data until 30 April. Shout if I misunderstood!”

  2. 2The Shared Ledger

    Keep decisions in one shared place everyone can see: the project's decision log, the ticket, the meeting notes. Not your private notebook.

    incantation“I've added it to the Lumen decision log, item 14, so the team sees the change too.”

  3. 3The Gentle Mirror

    When it happens, show the record without accusing. Curious, not triumphant.

    incantation“I may have misread it. Here's my note from the 9th, I'll forward it. Shall we decide now which comes first?”

VForbidden spells

Tempting. Effective for about a day. Each one has a soul cost = one ember of your soul.

  • The Secret Recording

    Recording calls without consent. In many places it's illegal, everywhere it's a trust bomb, and you will be the story instead of them.

  • The Gotcha Reply-All

    Forwarding the proof to the whole project with “as you can see below”. You win the argument and lose every future conversation.

  • Doubting Yourself Into Silence

    Deciding you must have got it wrong, again. This is what feeds the Wraith. Write it down instead.

VIDifficulty

The same creature at three strengths. Pick the one you're facing.

Normal

A forgetful colleague. Recaps fix it within two weeks, and they will probably thank you for them.

Nightmare

A pattern under pressure: they rewrite history whenever something slips. Recap everything, use the shared ledger, and raise the pattern with your manager using dates, not adjectives.

Hell

Deliberate and targeted: it's making you doubt yourself, and it's affecting your review. Keep a private dated log, keep copies of the recaps, speak to your manager's manager or HR, and talk to someone outside work. This is a case, not a creature.

VIIILoot drop

⚿The Binding Recap

Sent within the hour of any spoken instruction. Friendly tone, clear facts, an invitation to correct.

Subject: Quick recap: our chat this morning

Hi Rhona, thanks for the quick chat. Writing it down so I don't
get it wrong:

  2. Lumen meter data is top priority until 30 April

IXThe trial

What would you do? Tap a question for the answer.

Your manager gives you a new priority in passing at the coffee machine. What do you do next?

The Binding Recap, within the hour. Not because you distrust them, but because coffee-machine priorities are the first to be forgotten.

In a meeting, the Wraith says “I never said that.” You have the recap. What now?

The Gentle Mirror: “I may have misread it, my note says X, I'll forward it.” Then move the meeting on to deciding what happens now. Win the decision, not the argument.

The Wraith never replies to recaps. Does the recap still count?

Yes. A dated recap they received and didn't correct is a strong record. Keep it in the shared ledger as well, so it isn't just between the two of you.
