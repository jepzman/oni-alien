The Meeting Lich

Undead. Recurring. Spawns more of itself.

Threat
☠☠☠☠☠
Habitat
the 27th floor meeting rooms, every Tuesday at 10:00, forever
Weakness
a written agenda and a named decision

IA sighting

Gerald, Director of Alignment, runs the Weekly Sync on the Syncs: a 90-minute meeting where the leads of four other weekly meetings report on what was said in those meetings. It has met 112 times. It has made one decision, in 2024, which was to move itself from Monday to Tuesday.

Pip was invited “for visibility” in week 2. By week 6 he had been made owner of a follow-up meeting to align the actions from the sync. The Lich had reproduced. Pip now had four hours of meetings a week about one spreadsheet.

IIKnow thy enemy

A Lich is a meeting that has lost its soul (its purpose) but keeps walking. Meetings are cheap to create and expensive to kill: nobody wants to be the person who cancelled the thing the VP once attended.

The organiser usually means well. A recurring meeting feels like control. It looks like work on a calendar. And for someone whose job title contains the word “Alignment”, a full calendar is the evidence that they exist.

— the AnnotatorDo the maths out loud once. Twelve people, ninety minutes, weekly: that's 936 hours a year. MetroVolt bills a field engineer at €85 an hour. The Sync costs about €80,000. It has decided one thing.

IIISigns and omens

Tick two or more and you are in its lair.

  • No agenda, or an agenda that says “updates”.
  • The meeting ends with “let's take that offline” and a new invite appears an hour later.
  • More than eight people, and two of them are speaking.
  • Nobody can say what was decided last week.
  • It has outlived the project it was created for.

IVCounter-spells

  1. 1The Agenda Rune

    Before accepting, ask what decision the meeting needs from you. A Lich cannot survive a question about its purpose.

    incantation“Happy to help. What do you need me to decide or bring? Then I can prepare properly.”

  2. 2The Written Offering

    Replace your attendance with a written update. Most status meetings are a document read aloud.

    incantation“I can't make Tuesday, but here's my update in three lines. Shout if anything needs discussing.”

  3. 3The Last Rites

    At the end, say the decision and the owner out loud and write them down. Meetings that produce decisions stop needing to repeat themselves.

    incantation“Before we go: so we've decided X, Sam owns it, by Friday. Anything else, or can we give back the last ten minutes?”

  4. 4The Mercy Kill

    Propose ending the series, with a review date. People agree to “pause and see” more easily than to “cancel”.

    incantation“Shall we pause this for a month and switch to a written update? If we miss it, we bring it back.”

VForbidden spells

Tempting. Effective for about a day. Each one has a soul cost = one ember of your soul.

  • Laptop Necromancy

    Attending in body while openly working on something else. Everyone sees, nobody says, and you are now the person who doesn't care.

  • The Silent Decline

    Just stop showing up without a word. You will be discussed in your absence, which is the one thing worse than being in the meeting.

  • The Rival Meeting

    Fight the Lich by scheduling a meeting about the meeting. Congratulations. You are a Lich now.

VIDifficulty

The same creature at three strengths. Pick the one you're facing.

Normal

One drifting meeting. Ask for an agenda and suggest a shorter slot. Most organisers are relieved.

Nightmare

A meeting culture: your week is 60% recurring invites. Audit your calendar, decline with written updates, and agree with your manager which meetings you truly own.

Hell

Leadership measures commitment by attendance. Protect two focus blocks a week, keep your updates sharp so your absence looks efficient, not lazy, and pick your battles.

VIIILoot drop

⚿The Decline With Grace

A decline that keeps the relationship and the information flowing. Copy, paste, adjust.

Hi Gerald, thanks for the invite. I'm protecting focus time this
quarter for the Q3 forecast, so I'll skip the live session.

My update:
  • Forecast v4 shipped to Ops on 12 Mar
  • Next: Substation 14 capacity check (due 21 Mar)
  • Blocker: none

If there's a decision you need from me, send it my way and I'll
reply the same day.

IXThe trial

What would you do? Tap a question for the answer.

You're invited to a recurring 60-minute meeting “for visibility”. What do you send?

The Agenda Rune: ask what they need from you. If the answer is “nothing really, just to stay informed”, offer to read the notes instead.

Your own team meeting has started to drift. How do you tell?

Ask yourself: what did we decide last week? If nobody can answer, it's a Lich. Add a decisions section to the notes, or shorten it.

A VP attends the Sync once a quarter. Can you kill it?

Not on your own. Propose the Mercy Kill (pause with a review date) to the organiser and let them take it to the VP. Gerald gets to own the efficiency win.
