The Darkside of the Corporation · Act I

The Court of Whispers

Where power really lives, and how to walk its halls without being eaten

You were told that MetroVolt is run by an org chart. It is not. The org chart is a map of where the chairs are. Power lives somewhere else: in who gets a meeting on short notice, whose email gets a reply in four minutes, and who was in the corridor when the decision was made.

Many good people refuse to “play politics”. That is like refusing to “play weather”. It rains on you anyway. Politics is simply how decisions get made when there are more people than chairs. You can take part in it cleanly: by understanding it, building real relationships and being useful. That is all this Act asks.

— the AnnotatorClean politics: help people get what they need, in the open, and keep your promises. Dirty politics: everything in the Forbidden Spells sections. One builds a career. The other builds a story people tell about you after you leave.

This is the Act where Pip learns that being right is the beginning of the work, not the end of it. It is autumn at MetroVolt. Budget season. A reorg is rumoured. The corridors on 27 are very quiet, which is how you know something is happening.
