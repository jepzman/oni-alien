The Darkside of the Corporation · Act VII

The New Plagues

Fresh horrors for a new age

The old horrors (the credit vampire, the micromanager, the reorg) are still with us. But every age breeds its own plagues, and ours has bred some beauties.

Badge counts. Green dots. A machine that may or may not want your job. Four hundred Slack channels. A strategy that changes every quarter and a town hall that explains it in words that mean nothing. None of them is anyone's fault, exactly. All of them are yours to survive.

— the AnnotatorNew plagues spread fastest where nobody admits they're new. Name them, and half their power goes.

It's May 2029. Pip is back at The Grid after ten months at GridSight, as Principal Analyst, Customer Insight. Asha stays on at GridSight as its delivery lead. Rupert Thorne, the parent group's Transformation Director, has returned with a new programme, Ascend 2030, and a new AI assistant, VoltMind, for everyone. Floor 22 is closing. Something called “Collaboration Tuesdays” is coming.

Decrees, dots, machines and pivots 12 entries · ★ flagship

  • IThe Return-to-Office DecreeA proclamation from on high: thou shalt be seen, three days a week, mostly on video calls with people at home.
  • IIThe Green Dot of DoomA small green light that says you exist. Remote workers worship it. Managers watch it. It measures nothing.
  • IIIThe AI Replacement Prophecy ★A prophecy that the machine will take your job. Half true, half hype, entirely terrifying at 3 a.m.
  • IVThe Copilot Credit GrabThe machine wrote it. Someone prompted it. A third person presented it. Who gets the credit?
  • VThe Cult of Family“We're a family here.” Lovely, until the family restructures you.
  • VIThe All-Hands Mass ★Once a quarter the faithful gather to hear the liturgy: exciting times, doubling down, our journey. Nobody knows what it means.
  • VIIThe Engagement Survey OracleIt promises your answers are anonymous. Your team has five people. Your manager can read.
  • VIIIThe Hot-Desk Hunger GamesTwo hundred tributes, a hundred and forty desks, and a booking app that opens at midnight on Sunday.
  • IXThe Transformation Journey ★Every few years the kingdom sets out on a holy pilgrimage to a new way of working. It's the fourth one. The road looks familiar.
  • XThe Slack Channel NecropolisFour hundred and twelve channels. Twelve are alive. The rest are tombs that still send notifications.
  • XIThe Performative Busy-ness DanceAn endless dance of early emails, packed calendars and loud sighs. Very tiring to watch. Produces almost nothing.
  • XIIThe Pivot Storm ★The strategy changes every quarter. The wind blows a different way each time. The work stays half-finished in every direction.

How to read an entry

  1. A sighting: the creature in the wild, at MetroVolt Electric Corporation, scene by scene.
  2. Origin: how it came to be. Most monsters were bitten once, and the company rewarded what happened next.
  3. Know thy enemy: what it wants, why it thrives, what it fears, and its three breeds.
  4. Signs and omens, counter-spells (with the exact words: the incantation) and the forbidden spells you must not cast.
  5. Difficulty (Normal, Nightmare, Hell): how to tell which you're facing, and what to do at each.
  6. Are you the creature? A mirror. Then the rehearsal (the counter-spells, said out loud) and the trial.
