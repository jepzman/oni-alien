The Darkside of the Corporation · Act VIII

The Inner Sanctum

The final boss is you, at 2 a.m., checking email

Seven Acts of monsters, lords, councils and plagues. Here is the last secret of the grimoire: none of them is the final boss.

The final boss is quieter. It's you at 2 a.m., checking email. It's the job that slowly became your whole self, the line you crossed because someone senior asked nicely, the “yes” you said when you meant “no”. This Act is about keeping the one thing no reorg can restructure: you.

— the AnnotatorSome of these chapters touch on health, law and serious wrongdoing. The grimoire is a survival guide, not a doctor or a lawyer. If you're unwell, see a doctor. If something's unlawful or unsafe, get proper advice; whistleblowing protections vary by country.

It's March 2030. Ascend 2030 is a year old. Hugo, who has danced harder than anyone, is sitting in his car in the MetroVolt car park, unable to go in. Rupert Thorne has a board meeting coming and a number he'd like to be bigger. And the Chronicler, who has kept these notes since 2008, has just booked a meeting with HR called “Retirement: options”.

How to read an entry

  1. A sighting: the creature in the wild, at MetroVolt Electric Corporation, scene by scene.
  2. Origin: how it came to be. Most monsters were bitten once, and the company rewarded what happened next.
  3. Know thy enemy: what it wants, why it thrives, what it fears, and its three breeds.
  4. Signs and omens, counter-spells (with the exact words: the incantation) and the forbidden spells you must not cast.
  5. Difficulty (Normal, Nightmare, Hell): how to tell which you're facing, and what to do at each.
  6. Are you the creature? A mirror. Then the rehearsal (the counter-spells, said out loud) and the trial.
