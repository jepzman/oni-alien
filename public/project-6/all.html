Making It Yours

Tailoring a whole project, the city launch playbook, programmes and portfolios, benefits realisation, models and methods, artefacts, AI in project work, sustainability, the PMO, servant leadership, ethics, growing, hiring and handing over.

Fitting it to the work size the method, write the recipe, group the projects, check the benefits

  1. 01How Light Can We Go? + goes deeperSet the whole method for one project on one page: keep, shorten, drop, and check the fit.p. 01
  2. 02Can a Launch Be a Recipe? + goes deeperTurn repeated projects into a versioned playbook: fixed, local, a kit, and changes after every launch.p. 02
  3. 03One Project, or Many?Run projects that share a benefit as a programme; choose what to fund as a portfolio.p. 03
  4. 04Did We Get What We Paid For?Check each benefit, by its owner, on dates booked before the project closes.p. 04

Models, methods, artefacts choose tools by the question, keep the documents that are used, use AI and think long-term

  1. 05Which Tools, When?Choose models and methods by the question the project must answer, not by name.p. 05
  2. 06Which Documents Earn Their Keep?Keep the documents that were used after they were written; make the rest examples.p. 06
  3. 07Can AI Help? + goes deeperUse AI for drafts, summaries and checks; check every fact, own every line, keep decisions human.p. 07
  4. 08What Does It Leave Behind?Decide what a project leaves running while it's still on paper, priced over its life.p. 08

Leading others an office that helps, a colleague you coach, and the truth under pressure

  1. 09Who Helps the Project Managers Now?A supportive office with few mandatory rules, measured by what it makes easier.p. 09
  2. 10Who Serves Whom?Coach what's new, delegate what's known, unblock, give credit, and don't grab the pan.p. 10
  3. 11What Would You Do?Never shade a report; offer the person under pressure a true version that helps.p. 11

Growing get better on purpose, hire well, hand over, and look back

  1. 12How Do I Get Better at This? + goes deeperA log, feedback, a weak thing, a stretch, someone to coach, and a standard.p. 12
  2. 13Who Do We Hire?A role card, a real work sample, a scoring sheet written first, and a mixed panel.p. 13
  3. 14Can Someone Else Run This?Move decision rights, tell everyone who calls, step out on a date, and stop answering.p. 14
  4. 15What Was It All For?The six principles revisited: the day each one changed a decision.p. 15
