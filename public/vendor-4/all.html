Delivery Under Fire

The watermelon report, a RAID log people read, scope through the side door, multi-vendor blame, the rival as adviser, the steering ambush, escalation, re-planning, test environments, defect triage, quoting the contract, protecting margin, cutover planning, go / no-go and wave 1 night.

Telling the truth status that isn't a watermelon, a risk log people read, and scope that creeps

  1. 01Why Is Everything Green Except the Truth?Status from measures, not moods: burn-up, test pass rates and reconciliation, so the colour can't be green outside and red inside.p. 01
  2. 02Is Anyone Reading the RAID Log?A short log of risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies, each with an owner, a date and a next step, reviewed every week in fifteen minutes.p. 02
  3. 03Where Did This Extra Work Come From?Catch the small requests that arrive by chat and corridor, count them, and route them through the change pool before they eat the plan.p. 03

Other people's moves a vendor's blame, a rival's review, an ambush, and going over heads

  1. 04Whose Fault Is the Billing Failure?When another vendor blames you, go to the evidence together, under the interface agreement, and fix the cause before the blame.p. 04
  2. 05What Do We Do When Our Rival Advises the Client?Be transparent within the contract, ask the sponsor to define the reviewer's scope, protect your IP, and let the evidence answer.p. 05
  3. 06What Happens When Steering Turns on Us?Don't fight a surprise slide in the room: ask for the data, promise an answer in 48 hours, and pre-wire every steering after that.p. 06
  4. 07When Do We Go Over Their Heads?Escalate up a ladder agreed in advance, tell the person first, bring a proposal not a complaint, and come back down quickly.p. 07

Holding the line re-planning, environments, defects, the contract and the margin

  1. 08How Do We Re-plan Without Lying?Re-plan from the critical path and real velocity, show the options (scope, date, people), and never hide the slip in the buffer.p. 08
  2. 09Why Do the Test Environments Never Work?Count the test days lost, book shared environments like a resource, and test what you can without them.p. 09
  3. 10Is It a Defect or a Change?A triage board with agreed definitions: a defect breaks the agreed criteria; a wish is a change, however it's labelled.p. 10
  4. 11When Do We Quote the Contract?Read the contract aloud rarely: calmly, with an offer, and only when the relationship can't settle it.p. 11
  5. 12How Do We Protect the Margin?Protect the margin by managing scope, staffing and change, never by cutting testing, quality or honesty.p. 12

The night the cutover plan, the go / no-go and wave 1

  1. 13What Happens on the Night?A minute-by-minute cutover runbook, rehearsed three times, with named roles, checkpoints and a point of no return.p. 13
  2. 14Do We Go?Go / no-go against criteria agreed weeks before, decided by the sponsor, with the dissent recorded.p. 14
  3. 15What Did the Night Teach Us?Run the night by the book, fix what the book didn't foresee, and write down what happened before anyone sleeps.p. 15
