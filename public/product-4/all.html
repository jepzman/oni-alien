Measuring & Learning

Tracking plans, funnels, cohorts, A/B tests, guardrails, listening loops, OKRs and knowing when to stop.

Instrumenting measure properly before you judge

  1. 01Event Tracking PlanOne table of events: name, when it fires, properties, owner, the same on every platform.p. 01
  2. 02FunnelsCount users at every step to see where they drop out, before guessing why.p. 02
  3. 03Cohorts & Retention CurvesCompare groups that share a starting point to see whether customers come back.p. 03
  4. 04Dashboards That Get UsedOne page with the north star, its inputs and guardrails, reviewed weekly.p. 04

Experimenting test changes fairly, and read the results honestly

  1. 05A/B TestsSplit customers at random, change one thing, and compare on the metric you chose in advance.p. 05
  2. 06Sample Size & Test DurationWork out how many and how long before you start, so the answer means something.p. 06
  3. 07Guardrail MetricsThe numbers that mustn't get worse while you chase the one you want to improve.p. 07
  4. 08Reading Results HonestlyNo peeking, no fishing in segments, ranges not points, and decisions you set in advance.p. 08

Listening hear support, operations and customers, regularly

  1. 09Support Tickets as DataTag tickets consistently and read them as a weekly signal of what hurts customers.p. 09
  2. 10Feedback Loops with OperationsA regular, easy channel from pickers and couriers to the team, with answers going back.p. 10
  3. 11Customer Advisory PanelA small, varied group of customers for depth and early reactions, not for votes.p. 11

Goals steer by outcomes, review honestly, stop what doesn't work

  1. 12OKRsAn objective in words and a few measurable key results per quarter, graded honestly.p. 12
  2. 13Vanity vs Actionable MetricsPrefer numbers that change decisions over numbers that only look good.p. 13
  3. 14Post-Launch ReviewCompare what you expected with what happened, and decide what to do next.p. 14
  4. 15Killing a FeatureRemove features that don't earn their keep, carefully and on purpose.p. 15
