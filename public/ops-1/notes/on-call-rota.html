Who Gets Woken Up?

TL;DR

An on-call rota shares the job of answering alerts out of hours. Each week has a primary who is paged and a secondary who backs them up. There's a short handover, a named person to escalate to, and rest and pay for the disruption. The aim is that the business is covered and that no single person is the pager.

1The situation Larder, April 2026

Until April 2026 Larder's on-call is one phone, Eli's, 52 weeks a year. In the week of 23 March he was on call and loaded at 160 %, so Ana swapped in. She had no list of what she was covering, no runbooks and nobody to call but Eli. Rotterdam opens on 27 April and will add a third store to the night.

  • One person can't be reachable forever without burning out.
  • Swaps happen in a hurry, with no handover.
  • Everyone else stays unable to help.

2Real-life analogy the village doctors' weekend rota

A village practice has six doctors and a weekend rota on the door: one weekend in six each. On Friday afternoon the doctor going off duty tells the next one about the two patients to watch. If the on-call doctor is stuck, there's a named senior to phone. After a night of call-outs, they start late on Monday. Nobody thinks this is luxury. It's how the village keeps its doctors.

3How it solves it a rota, a pair, a handover, an escalation and rest

Eli asks for volunteers at tech leads on 16 April and gets four: Ben, Cho, Dee and Ana. With him that's five, one week in five each, starting Monday 20 April, a week before Rotterdam opens. Each week has a primary (paged first) and a secondary (next week's primary, who learns along the way). Eli is the escalation point for the first two months, until the runbooks exist.

The handover is Monday 10:00, 15 minutes: open issues, changes planned this week, anything flaky. Marco agrees €200 a week for the primary. The rule everyone likes best: paged after midnight, start at noon. The rota covers the critical and important tiers in the catalogue. Normal-tier services wait for office hours.

  1. Decide what on-call covers

    The critical and important tiers, not everything.

  2. Find at least four people

    Fewer than four, and it isn't a rota.

  3. Pair a primary with a secondary

    The secondary is next week's primary.

  4. Hold a short handover

    Open issues, changes, weak spots: 15 minutes.

  5. Name an escalation

    Who to call when stuck, and that calling is fine.

  6. Pay for it and protect rest

    Money for the week, time back after a bad night.

Vocabulary for the exam

On-call
Being reachable to respond to alerts outside working hours.
Primary
The person paged first.
Secondary
The backup if the primary doesn't answer, or needs help.
Handover
A short meeting where one on-call person briefs the next.
Escalation
Calling in someone more senior or more specialised.
Time off in lieu
Rest given back after out-of-hours work.

6Common pitfalls the usual ways this goes wrong

  • A rota of two

    That's 26 weeks a year each: still burnout. Fix → at least four people.

  • No runbooks yet

    New people are paged into things only one person understands. Fix → write runbooks for every paging alert (this volume).

  • Unpaid, unspoken

    Resentment grows, then people leave. Fix → agree pay and rest before the first week.

  • Swaps only in chat

    The tool pages the wrong person. Fix → swap in the schedule.

7When to use it (and when not)

Use it when…

  • One person carries the pager.
  • Before a launch that adds load at night.
  • Someone has swapped in without a handover.
  • People are afraid to take a week.

Skip it when…

  • Nothing you run needs a response before morning: say so and write it down.

8Self-check quiz

What does the secondary do?

Backs up the primary if they don't answer or need help, and learns, since they're next week's primary.

Why at least four people?

Fewer means being on call a quarter of the year or more: too much to last.

What goes into the handover?

Open issues, changes planned this week, and anything flaky.

What's Larder's rule after a bad night?

Paged after midnight → start at noon.

Which services does Larder's rota cover?

The critical and important tiers in the catalogue; normal waits for office hours.

what would you do?Ben is primary and gets paged three nights in a row. What should happen?

He swaps out after the second bad night, as agreed, and the alerts that paged him go to the next alert review: three nights in a row is a signal, not bad luck.

try it

Write down who can be called tonight if your most important service stops. How many names are there? Draft a rota with a primary, a secondary, a handover time and a rule for rest.

remember!!

On-call is a rota, not a person. If one name is always on it, the system depends on one person's sleep.
