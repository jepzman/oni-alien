Recovering what matters most, restoring it, working without it, and practising

  1. 01What Matters Most If Everything Fails?A business impact analysis gives each service a recovery time and a data-loss limit, set by what its loss costs.p. 01
  2. 02Can We Actually Restore?A backup is only real once it has been restored, timed and checked, on a schedule.p. 02
  3. 03When the Picker App Is DownA practised way to keep working without the system: paper pick lists, hand dispatch and a clear switch-back.p. 03
  4. 04Breaking Things on PurposeGame days and small chaos experiments: practise failures in daylight with a plan, a stop button and a review.p. 04
  5. 05Who Decides to Invoke the Plan?A named crisis team, clear triggers and a decision clock for switching to continuity plans.p. 05
  6. 06The Plan Nobody Has ReadKeep continuity plans short, owned, tested and changed after every test, or they rot.p. 06

Security operations seeing attacks, handling them, and keeping access and keys clean

  1. 07Is the Alarm Real?Collect the few logs that show attacks, keep them as long as needed, and alert on what matters.p. 07
  2. 08When the Incident Is a Security IncidentThe incident process with extra steps: preserve evidence, contain, involve legal, and notify on time.p. 08
  3. 09Who Still Has Access?A quarterly review where each owner confirms who has access to what, and removes the rest.p. 09
  4. 10Rotating the KeysKeep secrets in a vault, know their owners and ages, rotate them on a schedule, and practise a full rotation.p. 10

Risk, money and vendors the bill, the risks, the auditor, the exit and the office

  1. 11What Does It Cost to Run?Service financial management: cost per service and per order, owners who see their bill, and waste removed monthly.p. 11
  2. 12Which Risks Keep Us Up at Night?An operational risk register with owners, scores, actions and triggers, reviewed monthly.p. 12
  3. 13Prove It to the AuditorCollect evidence as you work, so questionnaires and audits are answered from records, not memory.p. 13
  4. 14Leaving a VendorAn exit plan per critical vendor: data, steps, time and cost, written while you're still friends.p. 14
  5. 15When the Office Is the OutageLaptops, sign-in, the office network and email are services too: plan for losing them.p. 15
