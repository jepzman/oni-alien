Deciding What to Build

Prioritising compare ideas with evidence, and order them by time and value

  1. 01RICE & ICE ScoringScore every idea the same way (reach, impact, confidence, effort), then argue about the numbers, not the sponsors.p. 01
  2. 02Cost of DelayPrice each week of waiting and divide by duration, so short, valuable work doesn't queue behind long work.p. 02
  3. 03Kano ModelAsk customers which features are basic, more-is-better or delightful, instead of guessing.p. 03
  4. 04MoSCoW and Its TrapsMust, Should, Could, Won't, with a strict Must test, room to spare and the Won'ts written down.p. 04

Saying no protect the focus, keep the relationships

  1. 05Saying No Without Burning BridgesA clear no with the reason, what you're doing instead, a small yes and a date to look again.p. 05
  2. 06Handling the HiPPOTurn the most senior opinion in the room into a hypothesis, and test it.p. 06
  3. 07Opportunity CostEvery yes is a no to the next best thing; name that thing and its price.p. 07
  4. 08Feature Request TriageOne front door for requests and a weekly triage, so everyone gets an answer within a week.p. 08

Strategy & roadmaps one page of strategy, a roadmap people can trust

  1. 09Product Strategy on a PageWhere we are, who we serve, how we win, what we bet on and what we won't do, on one page.p. 09
  2. 10Now / Next / Later RoadmapThree columns instead of a Gantt chart: sure now, likely next, possible later.p. 10
  3. 11Outcome-Based RoadmapsPut the result you're after on the roadmap, with the features as current bets.p. 11
  4. 12Roadmap vs Release PlanKeep the why-and-order roadmap apart from the dated release plan, and update both.p. 12

Making the case size it, price it, and bet instead of promising

  1. 13Opportunity SizingSize a market bottom-up from customers you can count, not from a big top-down number.p. 13
  2. 14Business Case One-PagerProblem, benefit, cost, payback, risks and the ask, on one page someone will read.p. 14
  3. 15Bets, Not PromisesPresent plans as bets with confidence and signals, not promises you don't control.p. 15
