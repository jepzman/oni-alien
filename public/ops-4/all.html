Making promises what working means, how good is enough, and what we signed

  1. 01What Do Users Count as Working?Service level indicators: measure each service the way its users experience it, as good events over all events.p. 01
  2. 02How Good Is Good Enough?Service level objectives: a target per indicator, below 100 %, chosen with the business and reviewed quarterly.p. 02
  3. 03When Do We Stop Shipping Features?The error budget is the unreliability the SLO allows; a written policy says what happens when it runs out.p. 03
  4. 04What Did We Sign?An SLA is a contract with consequences; keep it looser than your SLO, define it precisely and report it automatically.p. 04
  5. 05What Do Teams Promise Each Other?Operational level agreements: the internal promises that make the external ones possible.p. 05

Measuring honestly availability, capacity, reports, dashboards and the users' own view

  1. 06How Available Were We, Really?Count availability from real requests and outside checks, with rules for partial outages and maintenance.p. 06
  2. 07Will Christmas Fit?Watch headroom on the few limits that matter, alert before they run out, and review capacity before every peak.p. 07
  3. 08The Monthly Service ReviewOne monthly report per service against its SLOs, and a short meeting with its owner that ends in decisions.p. 08
  4. 09Dashboards People Actually OpenFewer dashboards, each answering one question for one audience, and delete the ones nobody opens.p. 09
  5. 10Asking the UsersAsk stores, support and clients how IT feels to them: uptime can be green while people are unhappy.p. 10

Running the year vendors, self-service, a second country, switching off and a peak

  1. 11The Vendor Went QuietSupplier management for silent failures: watch what a vendor should send, review vendors quarterly, keep score.p. 11
  2. 12What Can the Stores Fix Themselves?Move common fixes to the people closest to the problem, with safe self-service and short guides.p. 12
  3. 13Operating in a Second CountryA new country changes support hours, languages, vendors, holidays and rules; list the differences before opening.p. 13
  4. 14Switching a System OffRetire systems on purpose: find users, announce, go read-only, keep the data you must, cancel, delete, update the catalogue.p. 14
  5. 15Running a PeakPrepare for the busiest weeks with a readiness review, a freeze, extra cover and a daily check-in.p. 15
