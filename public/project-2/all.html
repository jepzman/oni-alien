Starting, Steering, Closing

The Governance domain: the charter, who decides, phase gates, one plan, sourcing, baselines, running the work, honest status, change control, lessons, handover and closing.

✎Index page numbers match the notes

Starting agree why, who decides, and when the next step may begin

  1. 01Should This Project Exist? + goes deeperThe project charter: one page that says why, what for, who is behind it and within what limits.p. 01
  2. 02Who Decides What?A sponsor, a steering group and decision levels, written down before the first hard decision.p. 02
  3. 03May We Take the Next Step?Phase gates and go / no-go: agreed criteria, checked at fixed points, with three possible answers.p. 03

One plan one plan everyone works from, and what was promised

  1. 04What's the Plan? + goes deeperOne plan that makes scope, dates, money, people and risks agree with each other.p. 04
  2. 05Make It, Buy It, or Hire It?Decide what to do yourself and what to buy, and pick the contract that puts each risk in the right hands.p. 05
  3. 06What Exactly Did We Promise?A baseline is the approved version of the plan that progress and changes are measured against.p. 06

Running and steering do the work, see where it stands, change course on purpose

  1. 07Is the Work Actually Happening?A weekly rhythm that keeps seven workstreams moving and clears what blocks them.p. 07
  2. 08What's in the Way Right Now?An issue is a problem that has already happened: log it, give it an owner and a date, close it.p. 08
  3. 09Is Our Way of Working Working?Quality assurance checks the process; quality control checks the result. A project needs both.p. 09
  4. 10Where Are We, Really? + goes deeperMeasure against the baseline, forecast the end, and report a colour that follows written rules.p. 10
  5. 11Can We Change That? + goes deeperEvery change to what was promised is written down, sized, decided by the right person and then built into the plan.p. 11
  6. 12When Do I Take It Upstairs?Escalate early, with options and a recommendation, when a decision is above your limit.p. 12

Closing finish properly: learn, hand over, close

  1. 13What Did We Learn?Capture lessons while they are fresh, sort them, and put them where the next project will trip over them.p. 13
  2. 14Who Looks After It Now?Hand the result to the people who will run it, with everything they need and nothing left hanging.p. 14
  3. 15Are We Done?Close on purpose: results accepted, contracts and money settled, benefits handed to their owners, the team released.p. 15
