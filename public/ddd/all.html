Domain-Driven Design

Finding the domain what matters, what it's called, and where it ends

  1. 01Ubiquitous Language still coreOne vocabulary shared by experts and code, inside one context, and used everywhere.p. 01
  2. 02Core, Supporting & Generic Subdomains matters more nowSort the business into what makes you different, what supports it, and what everyone has.p. 02
  3. 03Bounded Context matters more nowDraw a border around one model, so each word means exactly one thing inside it.p. 03
  4. 04Event Storming matters more nowMap a business process with orange sticky notes and the people who run it, in one room.p. 04

Between contexts how models and the teams behind them connect

  1. 05Context Map still coreDraw every context and how each pair relates, including who depends on whom.p. 05
  2. 06Customer–Supplier & Conformist still coreDecide whether a downstream team gets a say in its upstream's model, or simply takes it.p. 06
  3. 07Shared Kernel & Partnership rethoughtShare a small piece of model between two teams that agree to change it together.p. 07
  4. 08Anti-Corruption Layer still coreTranslate another model at your border, so its words and quirks don't leak into yours.p. 08
  5. 09Open Host Service & Published Language built in nowOffer one documented, versioned interface and format to every consumer.p. 09

Building blocks the shapes inside one model

  1. 10Value Object matters more nowA small immutable type defined by its value, like Money or EmailAddress, that checks itself.p. 10
  2. 11Entity still coreSomething with an identity that lasts while its details change, like a customer or an order.p. 11
  3. 12Aggregate matters more nowA cluster of objects changed together through one root, which guards their rules.p. 12
  4. 13Domain Events matters more nowRecord that something the business cares about happened, in the past tense, for others to react to.p. 13
  5. 14Repository built in nowLoad and save whole aggregates through a collection-like interface, hiding the database.p. 14
  6. 15Domain & Application Services rethoughtPut rules that belong to no single object in a domain service, and use cases in an application service.p. 15

Keeping it honest modern habits that keep a model sharp

  1. 16Specification rethoughtName a business rule as an object you can combine, test and reuse for checks and queries.p. 16
  2. 17Make Illegal States Unrepresentable matters more nowUse types so a wrong combination of data can't even be built.p. 17
  3. 18Anemic Domain Model rethoughtData classes with all the logic elsewhere: when it's a smell, and when it's fine.p. 18
